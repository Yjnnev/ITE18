Broccoli

Description

Broccoli is a cruciferous vegetable with a green flower head and thick stem.

Ingredients

  • Broccoli: 1 head, chopped
  • Garlic: 2 cloves, minced
  • Olive oil: 2 tbsp
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  2. Add garlic and sauté for 1 minute.
  3. Add broccoli and cook for 5-7 minutes, or until tender.
  4. Mix cornstarch with water and add to the pan. Cook for an additional minute, or until the sauce thickens.
