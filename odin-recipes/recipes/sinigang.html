Sinigang

Description

Sinigang is a popular Filipino sour soup made with tamarind and various ingredients.

Ingredients

  • pork belly: 1.5 lbs, cubed
  • water: 5-8 cups
  • Tomatoes: 2 medium, quartered
  • Tamarind paste: 2 tbsp
  • Onion: 1 medium, quartered
  • Radish: 1 medium, sliced

Steps

  1. In a large pot, bring water to a boil.
  2. Add pork belly, tomatoes, onion, and tamarind paste. Simmer for 45 minutes to 1 hour, or until the pork is tender.
  3. Add radish and cook for an additional 5-10 minutes, or until the radish is tender.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot with steamed rice.
